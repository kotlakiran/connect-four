💡 HOW TO PLAY
--------------------------------------------------
1. Tap any available column to drop your chip into the lowest unoccupied slot.
2. Form an unbroken line of 4 chips horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
3. Block your opponent's lines before they complete four-in-a-row!
4. Outsmart your opponent with deep tactical positioning and claim victory!
