# Privacy Policy for Connect Four

**Effective Date:** October 2026

Connect Four ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you use the Connect Four mobile application (the "App").

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
- **No Personal Data Collection:** Connect Four does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Local Database:** All game progress, profile stats, achievements, and settings are saved locally on your device using Android Room and DataStore.
- **Online Multiplayer:** When playing online matches, anonymous identifiers or user-chosen nicknames are transmitted solely to coordinate match sessions via Firebase Realtime Database / Firestore.

---

### 2. Permissions
- **VIBRATE (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback when dropping chips or claiming victories. Can be disabled in Settings.
